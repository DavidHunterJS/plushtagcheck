Send your photos

Email orders@plushtagcheck.com with:

  • A photo of the front of each toy.
  • Both sides of the paper heart tag (the swing tag), and both sides of the sewn-in fabric tag.
  • Your Stripe receipt number, or the email address you paid with.

Clear daylight photos work best. For a box of up to 25, you can send them over several emails.

We'll email your report within 48 hours of receiving the photos. If we can't identify a toy or find comparable sales, we'll refund it in full.
