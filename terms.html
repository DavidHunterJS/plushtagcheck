What you're buying

A written report, sent by email, that identifies your toy's tag generation and summarizes recent completed eBay sales of comparable toys. $9 covers one toy; $19 covers up to 25 toys from one person.

What it is not

The report is price research, not a certified appraisal or authentication, and it doesn't guarantee what your toy will sell for. Rare versions, such as the royal blue Peanut, need professional authentication before they're sold.

Timing and refunds

  • We send your report within 48 hours of receiving your photos.
  • If we can't identify a toy or find comparable sales, we refund it in full.
  • If you change your mind before sending photos, email us for a full refund.
