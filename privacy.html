Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

What we collect

  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We see your name, email and the amount, but never your full card number.
  • Photos and emails you send us: used only to identify your toys and write your report.
  • The quiz: runs in your browser. We record only which result you reached, not your individual answers.
  • Site analytics: we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count page visits, quiz results, clicks on the order buttons and completed orders. It doesn't give us your name or email. You can block it with Google's opt-out add-on or your browser's tracking protection. If you arrive from a Google ad, Google may also record that visit.

What we do with it

  • Your photos are deleted 30 days after we send your report.
  • We do not run your photos through AI tools and do not share them with anyone.
  • We never sell your information. We email you only about your order.

Contact

To have your photos or emails deleted sooner, write to orders@plushtagcheck.com.
