Is your old Beanie Baby worth anything?

Check the paper heart tag in about 60 seconds. You'll get its era and an honest answer, based on what buyers actually paid on eBay, not what sellers are asking.

The honest part first: most Beanie Babies sell for a few dollars. Even the famous Princess bear usually sells for about $10. The real money is in a small number of early tags and specific versions, and this check tells you whether yours might be one.

Things that do not make it valuable

  • "1993" on the sewn tag. That's the design's copyright date, not when your toy was made.
  • Spelling mistakes in the poem or tag. Late-1990s tag errors usually sell for the same few dollars.
  • "Made in Indonesia" or "PVC pellets" by themselves. These help identify a version, but on their own they're common.
  • Asking prices. Listings at $10,000 aren't sales. Only completed sales count.
