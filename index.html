Doesn't make it valuable

  • "1993" on the sewn tag

    That's the design's copyright date, not when your toy was made.

  • Spelling mistakes

    Errors in the poem or tag from the late 1990s usually sell for the same few dollars.

  • "Made in Indonesia" or "PVC pellets"

    These help identify a version, but on their own they're common.

  • A $10,000 listing

    Asking prices aren't sales. Only completed sales count.
